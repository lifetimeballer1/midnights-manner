# The Village Holds

Home defense posts are jobs: gates offer two slots, towers two, and the hall three. Available fighters fill them every five simulated seconds; ranged gear favors towers, frontline fighters favor gates. People → Defense job selects automatic assignment, a manually locked post, or an unstationed reserve. Select a defense and open Defense jobs to station or release fighters. Direct movement/attack/hold orders take priority. Ruined automatic posts relocate in peace; manually chosen posts remain locked for repair.

Raid warnings muster defenders at their posts. Posted fighters intercept local threats with two-second target commitment and distributed target scoring. Hall reserves and defenders at ruined posts can reinforce a nearby breach; other posts retain local coverage. Combat decisions plan at most twice per simulated second, with existing movement and damage ticks unchanged. Unposted fighters retain legacy engagement behavior. Archers that successfully retreat do not immediately approach again in the same tick. Raid results count newly breached defenses and responding fighters.

Civilians walk to a reachable exterior doorway, then disappear inside a living hall or housing building. Sheltered civilians have no mesh, shadow, footsteps or hostile target. Destruction removes protection immediately and occupants seek another shelter. Existing safe emergency repair/healing duties remain; carried goods, orders and workplaces survive alarms. Interior occupancy is runtime state, not a new housing limit or rendered interior. Strict routing has at most four searches per emergency tick, shared topology/threat sheets and retry backoff.

Staffed equipment workshops automatically request the highest unlocked compatible crafted tier needed by living villagers plus a configurable spare target (default one, maximum five). Ownership, stored equipment and active queues count against demand. Workshops wait for that tier's materials rather than silently forging lower tiers. Existing recipes, discounts, unlocks, durations and manual orders remain authoritative; equipment still requires fitting in People. Auto-craft can be disabled per workshop. New automatic orders wait during alarms; existing orders pause while all their workers are unavailable and resume without another payment.

Builders automatically travel to repair damage, prioritizing the hall, defenses and other buildings. Repairs, including ruins, restore up to six HP per second per available builder at 15 HP per wood, using the existing supply bonus. They then attend ordered construction. Base construction continues; builder equipment speed bonuses apply at the site once automation is configured. Assigned workplaces, manual orders, emergencies, expeditions, cargo and active hauling take priority over builder jobs.

Automatic upgrades require both the Stores master switch and a per-building Auto-upgrade toggle. Choose a maximum tier in the inspector. Builders finish repairs and construction first. At most one automatic upgrade starts at a time, using existing prices, gates, effects and build times. New upgrades wait during alarms. Stores → Protected resource reserves specifies floors for automatic crafting, automatic repairs and automatic upgrades; manual spending retains its existing behavior. Automatic settings are saved additively in v15. Runtime routes, targets, builder animation and interior occupancy restart on load without losing resources.

## Performance and verification

No runtime dependencies, additional units, interior geometry or extra navigation tiles. Tactical planning is staggered from movement, equipment/building planning runs every two seconds, caches stay runtime-only, and exterior visibility is resolved once per rendered frame. Existing overview geometry/effect limits remain. Focused tests cover assignment, targeting, shelter safety, automation budgets and portable saves.

Run `npm test`, `npm run build`, `npm run test:browser`. Optional CPU comparison: `CANVAS_MODULE=/absolute/path/to/@napi-rs/canvas/index.js node scripts/defense-benchmark.mjs`. Container Canvas timings are not iPhone FPS or sustained Safari heat measurements. Real-device Safari and home-screen app review remains required to establish device-specific performance.
