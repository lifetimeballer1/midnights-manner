# Target Economy Sheet — Midnights Manner

Design targets for tuning. Competent-player goals, not hard caps.

## Per-minute income bands (60s measure, workplaces assigned)

| Level | Food | Wood | Gold |
|-------|------|------|------|
| 1 | 8–14 | 6–12 | 4–8 |
| 2–3 | 14–22 | 12–20 | 8–14 |
| 4–5 | 18–28 | 16–26 | 12–20 |
| 6–7 | 22–34 | 20–32 | 16–26 |
| 8–9 | 26–40 | 24–38 | 20–32 |
| 10–11 | 28–45 | 26–42 | 22–36 |

## Mid-game throttle (implemented)

Full rates until 300s, linear ease to floor by 600s (passive 0.75, collectors 0.85). Upgrade time eases with the same curve when game.js is patched.

## Rules

Tune rates/costs before rewards. Level caches ≤ 3–5 min of target income at that level.
