# Target Economy Sheet — Midnights Manner

Design bands for active home play, not hard caps or measured player results. Rates are settlement totals in goods/minute; full nodes, full reserves, crew availability and player collection change actual throughput.

Task A's maximum-base-count sustained reference is 2,685 food, 1,320 wood and 1,350 gold/min before supply bonuses, refining and daily draws. Its full-node peaks are 4,392 / 2,088 / 1,998; these are bursts, not the sustained target. Collectors are excluded from this reference, so these figures are not total banked income.

## Developed-town spending bands (Task E baskets)

One active day is 180 seconds. At 60 mouths with Grand Works supply, the daily baskets use 4,800 food (including the meal), 2,520 wood, 800 flour, 120 bread (including the meal), 300 gold, 120 lumber, 12 plate and 20 frostwood. Preserve these Task E prices.

| Measure | Target band / reference |
|---------|-------------------------|
| Daily food + wood draw | About 60–75% of Task A sustained food + wood at 60 mouths, before refining |
| Fully staffed processing at level 10–11 | 5 tier-2 mills: 360 net flour + 180 bread/min, using 900 food/min; 3 tier-2 sawmills: 360 lumber/min |
| Net food / wood / gold after processing and daily draws | About 200–400 / 450–600 / 1,200–1,400 per minute at 60 mouths, with 0–5% supply production bonus |
| Net flour / bread / lumber after daily draws | About 90–100 / 130–150 / 310–330 per minute at 60 mouths |
| First Renown from replenishment | About 4–11 minutes at that reference; later purchases grow 35% per level |
| Ordinary tier-3 upgrade | 8–10× base basket; existing flat tier extras remain additional |
| Tier-4 defense upgrade | 20–24× base basket; existing flat tier extras remain additional |
| Final Project stage | 8× base; roughly 50–65 minutes of net lumber for the two largest lumber works |
| Storage fill without discretionary spending | Food roughly 30–50 min gross; wood/gold roughly 45–100 min gross; daily draws extend this |
| Above-cap settling | Retain 0.01 of excess + 0.02 goods/second; only excess stock settles |

Three mills leave only 216 net flour/min, below the 267 flour/min Grand Works basket at 60 mouths. The late mill limit now grows to four at level 9 and five at level 10; lower limits remain unchanged. This adds paid processing capacity for the existing supply sink without reducing producers. Five mills cover the flour basket at 100 mouths, but food may still be short without collectors or further earned capacity. Coverage is conditional, and purchases compete with upkeep.

Renown's first basket is 4,500 gold, 2,000 food, 2,400 wood, 60 flour, 900 lumber, 120 bread, 120 plate and 240 frostwood. Flour stays modest because supply already consumes it. Tier 1 remains base and tier 2 double, without new extras. Production rates, other building limits, storage bases and settling coefficients remain unchanged. Full storage is still possible when discretionary spending stops; Renown, defenses and Projects provide competing spends rather than a guarantee of zero surplus.

## Mid-game throttle (implemented)

Full rates until 300s, linear ease to floor by 600s (passive 0.75, collectors 0.85).

## Reward bands

Level caches should cover 3–5 minutes of the relevant level's income band. These are design targets; this Task F pass does not change rewards. Rate references assume continuous processing, available input and output room; dashboard estimates and full-node peaks alone do not verify banked throughput.
